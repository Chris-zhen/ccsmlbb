How the Tryout Works

  • Rank-Based Tiers: High-rank players and lower-rank players are split into separate matches so everyone competes on an equal playing field.
  • Best-of-1 Knockout: Your temporary team must win a single match to advance, but losing does not mean you are automatically out.
  • The Lifeline Rule: Exceptional individual players on a losing team can be rescued only by the official scouts and given a second chance on a new team.
  • The Carried Filter: Winning does not guarantee your safety, as underperforming or silent players can be benched or swapped out for the next round.
  • Mandatory Communication: You must open your mic to shotcall and give info, as staying completely silent is an instant disqualification.

Official Scout Standards

📢 A Message from the Scouting Committee:

Your matches will be strictly monitored by a dedicated scouting team composed of highly experienced, Mythical Immortal amateur tournament players with global badges .

Please note that only the scouts hold the absolute power to grant you a Second Chance Lifeline . To ensure completely unbiased and fair judgment, you may be evaluated by a different scout player for each match , each an expert in their specific role.

They aren't just looking at your KDA — they are looking for advanced item counter-building, map awareness, draft flexibility, and positive leadership. Show them you can adapt and communicate, even if your team is losing.
